# Resumable business setup and sharing (VIS-35)

An approved OWNER/MANAGER opens `/provider/<businessId>` from the provider workspace. The server-authorized setup screen links business details, services, staff/assignments, business hours, policy and sharing. Checkmarks come from publication readiness, including service/staff/hour blockers; no staff step is marked complete before an active service exists. Approval and publication remain separate. Draft changes save to PostgreSQL and resume from another device. A clean approved business can configure and publish through these screens without database configuration edits.

## Details and schedules

The public business profile saves name, location, category, description and optional public contact email/international phone. Contacts do not change identity sign-in methods, verify a login contact or grant business access. Fields are explicitly labeled public. Slug remains the stable value chosen on application; timezone is the pilot's Asia/Jerusalem value, read-only. Existing non-Israeli businesses retain their stored zone and publishing review blocker. No confirmed instant or recurring schedule is silently converted. A local preview shows only saved public fields.

The existing catalog editor is reused, including service validation, price/currency pairs and historical snapshots. Staff resources have editable names, capacity (1–100), active state and active service assignments. Adding a resource does not create an identity or membership; private invitations remain VIS-33. Staff creation accepts a business-scoped optional UUID creation key plus a stored request fingerprint. Retries with the same body reuse the resource; changed payloads conflict. The client keeps its key until acknowledgement, blocks simultaneous submissions, and proves a lost response/retry does not duplicate staff. Keys/fingerprints are omitted from staff projections.

Business and staff schedule editors support multiple weekly intervals and editable open/closed date overrides. Provider edits are displayed in local business time; labels and direction follow Hebrew/English independently. Empty staff recurrence follows business hours, with date overrides still applied. Policy values are explicitly reviewed/saved; prefills do not silently activate policy. Invalid/failed saves preserve the draft, and schedule forms offer an explicit reload of saved values.

All writes require current approved owner/manager context and the publication configuration transaction. Capacity reductions cannot invalidate existing allocations. Deactivation or assignment removal expires affected temporary PostgreSQL holds, releases their owned Redis reservations after commit, and preserves confirmed records and snapshots. Another valid staff resource can keep the business ready/published. New reservation creation rechecks active staff assignment inside its transaction, closing the advisory-read/configuration-edit race. Raw database capacity guards remain authoritative. Full schedule/booking audit events remain VIS-39.

## Publishing and sharing

VIS-34 readiness is reused rather than duplicated client-side. Only the server can publish. The private sharing endpoint uses the validated server `WEB_APP_URL` and the immutable `/p/<slug>` contract from VIS-45; it does not infer a production hostname from the request or a client redirect. Unpublished/unapproved/deleted businesses return no sharing URL. Invalid/unavailable sharing states keep actions disabled. Unpublishing hides the active QR/link and invalidates personal saved-list projections; previously printed links resolve through normal publication checks.

The sharing panel copies the exact canonical URL, exposes system sharing where supported, and renders/exports a QR with a four-module white quiet zone. QR modules and the downloaded SVG encode the same URL. QR generation is local with no external image service or credentials. Unsupported browser system sharing is visibly disabled. Native QR export uses Expo FileSystem/Sharing; native clipboard/SVG packages were installed through Expo's SDK-compatible installer. Actual device clipboard/share/installed-link behavior remains VIS-43. No in-app camera scanner or staff invitation QR is added.
