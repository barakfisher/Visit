# Home and navigation (VIS-46)

Ordinary sign-in always lands on Home for a unified identity. A signed-out visitor who opens Home is redirected to login once the session lookup has finished; a lookup still in progress or failed keeps the loading/retry state instead. Home shows the nearest upcoming confirmed appointment and the identity's saved providers in rows of two, plus the localized “Your time, well spent” heading only while the list is empty. All owned appointments are in the customer menu as “My appointments”. Empty state explains asking a business for its Visit link or scanning its QR with the phone camera. It never lists global businesses, invents providers or redirects based on global business counts.

## States and ownership

Home distinguishes session bootstrap, failed session lookup, provider-list loading, a successfully empty list, unavailable saved businesses and network failure. Account controls also wait for bootstrap, preventing a cookie-authenticated reload from momentarily offering guest-only preference writes. Retry requests the actual server state. Providers are paginated using the VIS-45 API; removal asks for confirmation, moves the provider to the archive (`/archived-providers`, linked below the list only when it has entries), invalidates that identity's list and clears its successful-activation marker. Removal also sends a compare-only expected-identity header, which the server rejects if the browser cookie changed to another account. It does not cancel or delete appointments. Private queries and drafts continue to clear on account/workspace/logout transitions; late transport responses fail their context-version check.

The Home appointment summary is a glance with one action, its details screen, where rescheduling and cancelling live. Home rechecks upcoming appointments periodically rather than freezing the next appointment at mount time.

## Menu and routes

The bottom tab navigator was replaced by an Expo Router Stack. The authenticated shell has a footer with the current mode's main destinations and a header with an avatar opening Account (application entry/status, sign out, the system administrator's block) and the approved provider-mode switch (decisions.md, 2026-11-08). Business mode shows a thin strip under the header, and Account opened from a business stays in business mode (`/account?businessId=<id>`). In provider mode Home opens the active business's agenda, the footer adds Add appointment and Team access, and Account adds Business settings. The account screen is canonically `/account`; `/two` safely redirects for old bookmarks. `/discover` still redirects to Home and performs no directory query. Authentication pages retain their focused login UI.

Capabilities come from the authenticated `/v1/me` projection, filtered to ACTIVE OWNER/MANAGER memberships in APPROVED businesses. Pending/rejected applications offer status and grant no management rights. Multiple approved memberships plus a pending application retain both valid options. With multiple businesses, mode switching requires an explicit business choice; it never guesses the first business. The profile must agree with the selected membership before navigation, and every backend provider endpoint still authorizes access. Customer-mode switching clears the selected workspace. Full revoked/invitation/provider route recovery is VIS-27, with VIS-33 as its prerequisite.
